I am actively searching for tenure track faculty positions—please reach out if your institution is hiring this cycle.

I study verified adaptation. Rather than yearn for a world where programs, architectures, and deployments hold still, I build hands-off programming languages, type systems, synthesis engines, and verifiers that permit systems to embrace the natural ebb and flow of software and hardware, while still providing strong guarantees about system behavior.
